AxomRank

Free, bilingual (English and Assamese) preparation for Assam government exams. Short lessons, real previous-year questions and source-checked Assam history, made for aspirants across Assam.

Open the free app Read the blog

Who AxomRank is for

  • ADRE (Assam Direct Recruitment Exam) Grade III and Grade IV aspirants
  • APSC CCE (Assam Civil Services) Prelims aspirants
  • Assam TET candidates
  • Assam Police SI and Constable aspirants
  • Students, teachers and anyone who wants to learn Assam's history, geography and culture

Why students use AxomRank

  • Free to start, no login needed. Open the app and begin a lesson.
  • English and Assamese together. Every lesson is written in both languages.
  • Works offline on ordinary Android phones, and can be installed from the browser like an app.
  • Built on the real exams. Our practice and analysis come from the actual question papers set by SLRC, APSC, SLPRB and the education department.
  • Remember, not just read. Short story-based lessons, 13 kinds of practice question and spaced review bring back what you are about to forget.
  • Source-checked. History lessons name their sources, and where sources disagree we say so.

Learn Assam's history

Ahom Kingdom History

The Ahom kingdom from 1228 to 1838: the story chapter by chapter, all 41 reigns on one timeline, the people and painted scenes of the chronicles, short films and a story map.

Ahom Kings lessons

41 short, game-like lessons, one for each Swargadeo, from Sukaphaa to Purandar Singha.

Assam Through Time

An interactive map that plays Assam's history year by year, from 350 CE to today.

Assam History Characters

328 people of Assam's history, from Pushyavarman to Tarun Gogoi, with portraits and scenes.

Assam Visual Archive

Scenes, community portraits, cultural objects and photographs of Assam.

Paintings and portraits on these sites are modern artistic reconstructions, not likenesses from the period.

Free resources for aspirants

  • Previous-year questions: 2,398 questions from 23 papers (ADRE 2022 and 2024, APSC CCE Prelims 2023–2025, Assam Police 2025, Assam TET 2021, DME 2025), with answers and topic tags. Each answer says whether it is from an official key or is our worked solution.
  • Syllabus collection: syllabi and exam patterns for 12 Assam recruitment boards.
  • Assam maps: districts, villages, constituencies and more, to explore or download.
  • The Floor Test: learn how parliamentary majorities work by playing.
  • AxomRank blog: exam analysis built from real papers, for example what 1,290 ADRE questions tell you and the list of all 41 Ahom reigns.
