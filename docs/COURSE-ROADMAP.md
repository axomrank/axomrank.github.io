## Cheaper than any new course

Geography has 186 past questions, but both Geography courses have empty question banks, and
so do Mathematics and Reasoning. Filling those banks serves learners sooner than a new course.

## Rules for every plan

- Past papers set patterns only. Never copy questions, and never label a question with an
  exam unless its provenance is verified.
- Read actual source passages before making factual claims. `AGENTS.md` applies in full.
- Any figure that can change carries an `asOf` date.
- New Assamese stays pending editorial review.
- Each course gets its own branch. Published lesson and step ids are permanent.
- Unit budgets follow the Reasoning rule: 20 or more past questions gives 6 units, 10 to 19
  gives 4, and fewer gives 3. Small scattered subjects use the syllabus instead, as noted.
