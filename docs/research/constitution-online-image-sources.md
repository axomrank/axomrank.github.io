# Constitution story cards with online visual sources

Research pass on 2026-11-05. This is a count of distinct card placements with a named asset or document, including the first ten-card shortlist. A source can serve more than one card where each placement has a separate teaching purpose. Generic courthouse and parliament photos were not counted across every card that mentions those institutions.

**Clear terms** means the source identifies the rights holder or releasing institution and lists reuse terms, or gives a public-domain basis. Apply the listed credit and any share-alike conditions. **Document** means an identifiable scan or official PDF. Select a legible excerpt and check the applicable reproduction terms before publishing. **Check** means the image has been located and has a concrete use, with provenance or permissions still to resolve.

For page scans, use a tight crop with a bilingual caption so the teaching point survives mobile size. A modern photo can establish where an institution sits. Historical event photos need a verified date and subject. The 435-card denominator comes from the picture inventory after the map and CSS pass. This source-matching pass establishes a lower bound on what is available online.
