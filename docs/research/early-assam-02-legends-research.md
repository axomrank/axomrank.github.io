## Visual decisions after research

Minimal cartoons: plain light backgrounds,2–3 large shapes/figures, simple outlines, restrained colours and no tiny scenery or text. Naraka's later phase is visibly fierce/arrogant and villainous, matching the attributed narrative of oppression; his infancy is not portrayed as inherently evil. Varaha is the boar form of Vishnu, depicted respectfully with boar head and divine human body. Naraka is not automatically given a boar head. Danava/Asura are mythic labels; a cartoon villain is not a depiction of a modern ethnic group.

No pig/boar flag is assigned to Naraka or Bhagadatta as a factual emblem. Where Naraka's banner is shown, use a simple thunderbolt motif based on the cited passage, with the exact flag shape/colour identified as artistic. Boar imagery belongs to the Varaha birth scene. Bhagadatta's scene needs no flag. Legends have no fabricated reign dates, invented dialogue or documentary claims. The bird in the stairway account is a cock/rooster.
