# Constitution course: story-card picture inventory

Measured from the 60 course lesson files on 2026-11-04. This is a structural inventory, not a claim that every card needs a unique image.

- 473 story cards in 236 units.
- 20 use bilingual CSS diagrams. 17 now use dedicated locator maps, reusing 11 map assets. Eight more use sourced photos or document scans. The remaining 427 use built-in art.
- One card uses a rendered map: `con-46.u3.01`, “Three names in Part I”. This is in addition to the 17 locator maps.
- All 60 lesson tiles use the same `constitution/part-tile.webp` placeholder.

Cards below are grouped by lesson and unit. IDs are permanent; preserve them when adding images. An art label identifies the current reusable illustration, not a proposed picture.

Prioritise historically identifiable people, documents, places and Assembly events, then geography and institutional processes where a map or diagram aids understanding. Reuse appropriate pictures across related cards; abstract provisions may work better as diagrams than photographs. Verify sources, reproduction rights and captions before use; distinguish artistic interpretation from evidence.
