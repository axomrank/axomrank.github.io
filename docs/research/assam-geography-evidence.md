# Assam Geography evidence and visual inventory

All site dots are locators. Administrative outlines and the northern river
layer reuse the repository’s simplified map data. The source KMZ and regional
boundary reuse terms require verification before public release.

| Mission | Evidence used | Visual treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Bearings | [Assam Directorate of Geology & Mining](https://dgm.assam.gov.in/about-us/history), location and physiographic description. [IWAI regional waterways](https://iwai.gov.in/north-east-region), river cities. | Existing India and Assam basemaps. The worksheet outline uses the filled district geometry with boundaries and labels omitted. |
| Rivers | [River pilot ledger](assam-geography-river-pilot.md) | Shared Brahmaputra geometry. Barak branches and drainage divide are teaching schematics. |
| Floodplain | [ASDMA and NRSC Atlas](https://www.nrsc.gov.in/nrscnew/assets/pdf/Flood_Hazard_Zonation_Atlas_of_Assam_using_multi_sensor_satellite_data1998_2023.pdf), observation period and introductory account of satellite mapping. | Original paired views of inundation and bank retreat, with a teaching legend. These are schematic rather than satellite captures or atlas classes. |
| Landscape | [IMD Guwahati climatological table](https://city.imd.gov.in/citywx/extreme/JUL/guwahati2.htm), 1991–2020 mean total rainfall for January 11.5 mm, July 283.4 mm and October 117.9 mm. [Ramsar sheet](https://rsis.ramsar.org/RISapp/files/RISrep/IN1207RIS.pdf), Deepor Beel location and character. [UNESCO Kaziranga](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/337) and [UNESCO Manas](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/338), habitat descriptions and property locators. | Course-drawn chart from three IMD rows and shared map with approximate conservation locators. |
| Livelihoods | [Assam DES publications](https://des.assam.gov.in/portlets/our-publications), agricultural and tea statistics series. [IndianOil](https://iocl.com/pages/digboi-refinery), refinery role and paraffin wax. [IWAI NW-16](https://iwai.gov.in/offerings/national-waterway/national-waterways16), route endpoints. | Original instructional plot comparison, approximate Digboi locator and schematic Barak route. Crop statistics are referenced for context. |
| Field case | Course-authored map and explicit scenario rules. | Original fictional market and assessment maps. Road and flood-zone information is available as text during the test. |

The bilingual draft uses course-authored schematics for the flood comparisons,
farming plots and field case. Adding rights-cleared dated flood imagery, a
Barak channel layer and local crop statistics would move those units closer
to the initial visual plan.

Mission 1 now uses the northeastern plateau and Purvanchal groupings from
[NCERT chapter 2](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/kegy102.pdf). The Malda
fault is the textbook separator; the [geophysical study of the Rangpur
Saddle](https://se.copernicus.org/articles/17/389/2026/se-17-389-2026.html)
treats the Garo–Rajmahal gap as the broader feature framed by separate faults.
The [Dima Hasao district profile](https://dimahasao.assam.gov.in/about-district/district-glance)
places Barail in the district. The [IGNCA historical geography scan](https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/16981.pdf)
supports the older Daphla, Miri, Abor and Mishmi labels.
