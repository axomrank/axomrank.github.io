# Follow the Water · evidence and visuals

- Brahmaputra direction and Barak passage through the Cachar Plain come from
  the river descriptions in the [Flood Early Warning System documentation](https://darpg.gov.in/sites/default/files/70.%20Flood%20Early%20Warning%20SystemFLEWS-Documentation-Final.pdf).
- Dibrugarh, Pandu (Guwahati) and Dhubri are listed in the
  [IWAI regional river-port description](https://iwai.gov.in/north-east-region).
  Their sequence also follows the westward course on the lesson map.
- Subansiri and Burhi Dihing use the north and south tributary lists in the
  [Assam Water Resources Department river description](https://waterresources.assam.gov.in/portlet-innerpage/brahmaputra-river-system).
- The [Sribhumi geography page](https://sribhumi.assam.gov.in/about-us/geography)
  describes the Barak dividing near Bhanga into the Surma and Kushiyara.
- The lesson uses existing simplified district and river geometry from
  `content/maps/assam.json` and city positions from `content/entities.json`.
  The map file credits the district and river geometry to
  `Dist + Rivers (simplified).kmz`. Original reuse terms still need verification.
  The Barak card locates Silchar on the shared map and uses a separate
  schematic for the river sequence. A sourced Barak channel layer is pending.
- The river cover is an original schematic illustration. Its branches do
  not represent surveyed channels. The river sequence and ridge diagrams
  are course-authored teaching schematics.
