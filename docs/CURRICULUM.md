# Curriculum: all of Assam's history

The History subject is one continuous path of courses from earliest Assam to today. Each course follows [LESSON-DESIGN.md](LESSON-DESIGN.md): eras → lessons → units, with a checkpoint at the end of every era. Tiers (A/B/C) set how long a lesson is; the exam weight decides the tier. Titles below are working names; dates are to be verified against sources when each lesson is written (every lesson lists its `sources`).

## Time budget

A learner preparing for ADRE, TET, APSC or SI should finish the whole of Assam History in about **25 hours** (≈ 370 units at ~4 minutes). Each course gets a unit budget from its share of exam questions, not from how much history there is to tell. A lesson may not grow past its budget; extra detail goes into the guidebook or the optional deep-dive course.

## What makes the cut (exam filter)

Applied to every course, and strictly to the Ahom period:

- **Keep:** firsts and founders; dates of founding, battles, treaties, annexations; capitals and their builders; offices and institutions; monuments and who built them; rebellions and their leaders; famous people with one line each; anything in a previous-year paper.
- **One line only:** kings with short or minor reigns, court intrigue, succession quarrels, genealogy beyond father/son of famous rulers.
- **Leave out of the path:** reign-by-reign detail that no exam asks; it stays in the deep-dive course and guidebook.

**How to build it without losing anyone's progress**

- New course `ahom-kingdom` with new lesson ids (`ahom-01-sukaphaa`…). Saved progress, mistakes and review are keyed by lesson id → step id, so the old course keeps working untouched.
- Write the new lessons by **choosing and trimming** steps from the old ones: keep the best story card and the exam-fact exercises per unit, drop repeats and minor-reign detail. New step ids (never reuse old ids in a new lesson). Media, maps and entities are reused as they are.
- The old course is renamed **"Ahom kings, reign by reign (deep dive)"**, shown after the exam path on the subject page with an "optional" pill. Its 41-king family tree and profile tabs stay; the compact course can link each king card to it.
- Learners who already finished old lessons are offered a **test-out** of the matching compact lessons (map in the course file: compact lesson → old lessons).
- Re-tag the 76 questions in `content/questions/ahom.json` to the new lessons, and add about 150 more, aimed at the must-know facts column.

## H5 · British period and freedom struggle (18 lessons, 75 units)

Highest exam weight; build first after H3.

- **Era 1 Annexation, 1826–1858** (4): David Scott and Company rule; hill resistance (U Tirot Sing 1829–33, Singpho, Khamti 1839); Gomdhar Konwar (1828); Maniram Dewan, Piyali Barua and 1857.
- **Era 2 Economy and administration** (5): tea, the Assam Company (1839), indentured labour; land revenue and the peasant risings: Phulaguri (1861), Rangia and Lachima (1893), Patharughat (1894); province changes: Bengal, Chief Commissionership (1874), Eastern Bengal and Assam (1905), Province (1912), Acts of 1919 and 1935; the Line System and immigration; railways, oil at Digboi (1889).
- **Era 3 Awakening** (4): American Baptists and the Orunodoi (1846), Anandaram Dhekial Phukan, Assamese restored in schools (1873); the Jonaki era (1889) and Bezbaroa; Assam Association (1903), Ryot Sabhas, Assam Chhatra Sanmilan (1916), Assam Sahitya Sabha (1917).
- **Era 4 Freedom movement** (5): Swadeshi in Assam; Non-Cooperation and Gandhi's 1921 visit, Assam Pradesh Congress (1921); Civil Disobedience; Quit India 1942 (Kanaklata Barua, Mukunda Kakati, Kushal Konwar, Bhogeswari Phukanani, Tileswari Barua); the war in the North-East (Kohima, Stilwell Road, INA); Cabinet Mission and Grouping, Gopinath Bordoloi, Sylhet referendum (1947).

## H6 · Assam since 1947 (11 lessons, 42 units)

Partition and refugees, the 1950 earthquake; reorganisation of the North-East (Nagaland 1963, Meghalaya 1972, Mizoram and Arunachal 1987); the refinery movement (1956–57); the language movements (1960, 1972); the Assam Movement (1979–85) and the Assam Accord (1985); AGP; Bodo movement and accords (1993, 2003, 2020), BTR; Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao councils and accords; insurgency and peace talks; NRC and CAA; chief ministers in order; landmark projects (Bogibeel, Dhola–Sadiya bridge).

## H7 · Religion, literature, art and culture (13 lessons, 55 units)

Sankardev (A); Madhavdev and Damodardev (B); satras and naamghars: Majuli, Barpeta, Auniati (B); Kamakhya and the Shakta tradition, Ambubachi (B); early literature: Charyapada, Hema Saraswati, Madhav Kandali, Ananta Kandali (B); Buranjis (C); modern literature: Bezbaroa, Padmanath Gohain Barua, Jyoti Prasad Agarwala, Bishnu Rabha, Bhupen Hazarika (A); Jnanpith and other awards (C); Bihu and festivals (A); Sattriya, Ojapali, Bhaona, Borgeet, Deodhani, tribal dances (B); silk (muga, pat, eri), weaving, bamboo and cane, GI tags (B); film and theatre: Joymoti (1935), mobile theatre (C); Assamese language and scripts (C).

## H8 · Sources, monuments and archaeology (6 lessons, 22 units)

Inscriptions and copper plates; Ambari, Da Parbatia, Madan Kamdev, Surya Pahar, Hajo; Sivasagar group (Rang Ghar, Talatal Ghar, Kareng Ghar), Charaideo moidams; chronicles and foreign accounts (Xuanzang, Minhaj-i-Siraj, Shihabuddin Talish, Mirza Nathan); museums and protected sites. Built mostly from H1–H3 entities and media: revision across periods.

## H9 · Peoples, councils and movements (8 lessons, 30 units)

Bodo-Kachari, Karbi, Dimasa, Mising, Rabha, Tiwa, Deori, Sonowal Kachari, Tai peoples, tea-garden communities; Sixth Schedule and autonomous councils; Inner Line, Line System, tribal belts and blocks.

## Build order

1. **H3 compact Ahom path** (content exists; mostly cutting): sets the length standard for every other course. About 2 weeks.
2. **H5** British period and freedom struggle.
3. **H7** culture, starting with Sankardev and literature.
4. **H6** Assam since 1947.
5. **H2** medieval kingdoms, then the four missing **H1** lessons.
6. **H8, H9** as revision courses from shared entities and questions.

Questions: about **2,500** exam-style questions across History (≈ 7 per unit), each tagged with exams and, when from a past paper, `pyq` with year. Update `content/catalog.json` as each course goes live (replace the "soon" rows).

## Content rules for every lesson
- Facts need a named source in `sources`; flag disputed dates in the lesson.
- Spelling of names follows official/most common exam usage; keep alternates in `entities.json` aliases.
- Every lesson adds to the shared `entities.json` (people, places, offices, terms) and the master timeline.
- Every question carries `exams` tags (adre, tet, apsc, si) and, if from a past paper, `pyq` with year.
- A lesson stays inside its tier's unit count; anything more goes to the guidebook.
